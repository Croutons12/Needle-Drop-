What this site stores

Spinlist saves your favorite albums in your browser’s local storage. If you connect Spotify, it also saves the Spotify app’s public Client ID in local storage and keeps your Spotify sign-in tokens in session storage for the current browser tab. Your Spotify catalog favorites, including their album details and artwork links, are saved locally so they remain in your crate after a refresh.

What Spotify can see

When you connect, Spinlist uses Spotify’s Authorization Code with PKCE flow and requests no additional account permissions. It sends your Spotify catalog search terms to Spotify’s Web API. Spinlist does not request or read your Spotify profile, playlists, or listening history. MusicBrainz genre searches use the separate Worker described below.

Music discovery

When the MusicBrainz Worker is configured, the genre you select is sent through that Cloudflare Worker to MusicBrainz to find albums. The Worker caches genre lists and album results to reduce repeat requests; it does not receive your saved favorites or Spotify tokens. Album-cover images are loaded from the Cover Art Archive when available. Missing artwork uses a generated cover instead.

Disconnecting and deleting

Choose Spotify settings → Disconnect to remove the Spotify session tokens and saved Client ID from this browser. Your saved album favorites remain until you remove them with the heart buttons or clear this site’s browser storage. Clearing the site’s local storage also removes the locally saved album details.

Third-party services

Spotify receives requests made to its authorization and catalog API services when you use Spotify features. MusicBrainz receives genre searches through the Cloudflare Worker, and the Cover Art Archive may serve album images. Google Fonts may receive a request to serve the site’s typefaces. Spinlist does not include analytics or advertising trackers.
